About

Our newest store opened mid-January 2014. It is a few miles down from SeaWorld at the corner of International Drive and Central Florida Parkway. We are located behind the Wawa next to AppleBeesl. We have outdoor patio seating with night lighting, indoor seating, and a drive-though for your convenience. We serve soft-serve ice cream using the same great ingredients as our two Twistee Treat locations. We are also now serving creamy hand-scooped ice cream in eight popular flavors.
