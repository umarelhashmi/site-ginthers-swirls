✨First visit to Ginther’s on a Sunday evening in March after a full day at a nearby theme park. We intentionally avoided spending on overpriced theme park treats and needless to say, so glad we did.

My teenage daughter ordered a banana boat (similar to the banana split, but vanilla ice cream exclusively) with all the toppings- hot fudge, whipped cream and they generously provided (her favorite) extra cherries at no additional charge.
I tried one of the seasonal, specialty flavor - pomegranate. It was very yummy … just the right combination of sweet with a hint of sour.

The customer service is five star 🌟🌟🌟🌟🌟 OUTSTANDING. It was spring break, so they were open an hour later. We arrived close to closing time and the staff were so upbeat, friendly and engaging. My teenagers portion was so huge I helped her finish it. This was the perfect ending to a very eventful day! Thank you 🙏🏽. We look forward to returning again to try some of the other flavors!!
