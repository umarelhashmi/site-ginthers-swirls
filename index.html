About Us

We are a family-owned small business operating two Twistee Treat locations and our newest Ginther's Swirls. Ginther's Swirls Ice Cream opened mid-January 2014. It is a few miles down from SeaWorld at the corner of International Drive and Central Florida Parkway. We are located behind the Wawa next to AppleBees. We have outdoor patio seating with night lighting, indoor seating, and a drive-though for your convenience.

We serve soft serve ice cream using the same great ingredients as our two Twistee Treat locations. We are also now serving hand-scooped ice cream in eight popular tasty flavors. Please see our menu for pricing.

Our products are made from real milk and cream served in waffle cones, sugar cones, and cups. We also offer fat-free, sugar-free vanilla soft-serve. We have dips, Flavorburst, sprinkles, and various toppings to add to your dessert. You can also choose milk shakes, malts, Twirls (shakes with chunks of candy or toppings), sundaes, splits, smoothies, floats, and Chillers. We can customize your order to suit your taste. Just ask!

Welcome to Twistee Treat of Ocoee, Twistee Treat of Lake Buena Vista, and Ginther's Swirls Website. We have been family-owned and operated since 1994. Twistee Treat of Ocoee and Twistee Treat of Lake Buena Vista are a direct licensee and have been granted the right to continue to use the name from Twistee Treat USA.
